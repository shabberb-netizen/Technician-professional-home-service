1. Material Provision
The technician is solely responsible for bringing all necessary materials and tools required to complete the service.
2. Punctuality
Technicians are expected to be on time. If there is any delay on the part of the technician, any agreed-upon compensation or financial adjustments are to be handled directly with them.
3. Off-Platform Transactions
The platform and app are not responsible for any issues, misconduct, or disputes arising from services arranged outside the app (e.g., dealing directly with a technician using their personal contact number). Only bookings made through the official app are protected.
