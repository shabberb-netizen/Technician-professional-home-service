Technician Professional Home Service - LOGIN.md
1. Authentication Options
 * Sign In/Sign Up: Via Google or Mobile Number.
 * Forgot Password: Recovery option via mobile number or email account.
2. Role Selection
After login, users are presented with two options to proceed:
A. Customer Authentication
 * Address entry.
 * GPS Location permissions.
 * Identity verification details.
B. Technician Authentication
 * Years of experience.
 * Work location.
 * Service Specialization (Electrician or Plumber).
 * Identity & Documentary verification.
 * Contact number.
 * GPS Location tracking setup for service routing.
